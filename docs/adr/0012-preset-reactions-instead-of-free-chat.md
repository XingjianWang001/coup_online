# Preset Reactions instead of free-text chat

Players get table talk through Reactions: preset Phrases, Announcements and Verbal Claims shown briefly on the sender's seat nameplate, not through a chat box. The site is ICP-filed and public in mainland China, and whether adding player-authored text there brings further filing, security-assessment or moderation duties is unconfirmed (open question 11 of the chat research on `feat/chat`, `docs/research/text-and-voice-chat.md`). Presets avoid that question entirely, along with chat history, sensitive-word filtering and muting (open questions 3 and 4). They also work across languages: the wire carries only a Phrase key, an action and target Player ID, or a role, and each browser renders the text in its own locale (ADR 0008). A Chinese and an English Player can read each other's Reactions, which free text cannot offer.

The server treats a Reaction as an Intent it validates and relays but never applies (ADR 0002): it accepts only preset content during a Game and rebuilds the Reaction from known fields, so nothing else a client sends is forwarded. It drops a second Reaction from the same Player within 1.5s; the client cools down for 2s. Reactions never enter Game state or the log and are not replayed on reconnect. They are non-binding and cannot be challenged, so an Announcement does not commit the Player to that action and a Verbal Claim is not a Claim.

**Considered Options**: Free-text chat was rejected for the compliance, moderation and language reasons above. Reactions aimed at another Player were rejected because the sender's seat is where the table looks, and context already tells who "You sure?" is aimed at. Emoji were rejected because they render differently per platform and their colour breaks the single brass accent. Reactions in the Lobby Phase were left out because there are no seats to show them on.

**Consequences**: Adding a Phrase means adding its key to `PHRASES` in `@coup/shared` and its text to both locales. Free-text chat stays a separate decision that must settle the compliance question first.
